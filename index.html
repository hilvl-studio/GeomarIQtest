1. Assessment Data

This assessment uses your name, phone number, designation, and answers during the current browser session. When you submit, only your name, phone number, and score are included in the request.

The configured submission endpoint is https://google.com. This address is not a Google Sheets webhook. The application cannot confirm delivery to HR or storage in Google Sheets, and does not control the destination's retention policy. Continue only if you consent to sending your name, phone number, and score to this address.

3. Camera Access

Camera access is required before the test can start. The webcam preview is local to your browser. This application does not record, upload, or perform AI analysis of the camera feed. The camera is stopped when the assessment ends.

4. Browser Storage & Navigation

Assessment answers are held in memory, not saved to browser storage. Refreshing or closing the page loses your progress. Switching tabs or applications does not invalidate or lock the test; the 45-minute assessment timer continues.

6. Consent

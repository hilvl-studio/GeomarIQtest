Privacy & Data Processing Agreement

1. Introduction

Geomar Gulf LLC ("the Company") is committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of all assessment candidates in accordance with applicable data protection regulations, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable regional equivalents.

2. Data Collected

During this assessment session, the Company collects the following categories of personal data: full legal name, contact phone number, cognitive assessment responses, session metadata, and webcam verification footage used solely for identity confirmation and proctoring integrity purposes.

3. Purpose of Processing

Your personal data is processed exclusively for the purpose of evaluating your cognitive aptitude as part of the Company's recruitment and selection process. Data will not be disclosed to third parties outside of the Company's authorized recruitment personnel without your explicit consent.

4. Data Retention

Assessment data is retained for a period of 90 days following the conclusion of the relevant recruitment cycle, after which all personal data is securely and permanently deleted in accordance with the Company's data retention and disposal policy.

5. Your Rights

You retain the right to access, rectify, restrict processing of, or request the deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting our Data Protection Officer at: privacy@geomargulf.com. Requests will be processed within 30 days in accordance with applicable law.

This application utilizes session-based browser local storage exclusively to maintain assessment integrity, enforce single-session access controls, and prevent unauthorized re-entry. No third-party tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or cross-site identifiers are deployed.

7. Webcam & Proctoring

Camera access is required to enable AI-assisted live proctoring during the assessment. Your webcam feed is used solely for identity verification and session monitoring. No video data is transmitted to external servers beyond the scope of this assessment session.

8. Consent Declaration
